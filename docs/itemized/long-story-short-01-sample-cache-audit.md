### Correction provenance

The prior AAO publication stopped at `2026-02-05`. Human review established
`2026-02-19` as the authoritative endpoint. Revision 2 regenerated the
weekly, cumulative, cumulative IP-analysis, and cumulative BTIHA-analysis
products from canonical cache archive `cache.20260219.tar.xz` (602,655,404
bytes; SHA-256
`a6eabc6484aed65a93f6913e1403a02f75fd0991ee6264bc2c7ab594496bce12`).
The exact prior release files remain preserved at
`release-history/2026-08-07/`.

### Evidence boundary

No raw sample was opened and no cache archive was mutated. The day bundle was
generated by read-only streaming of strict per-torrent day cache records;
hour, week, and cumulative cache members were excluded from that day-only
stream. The aggregate successor correction was separately authorized and
used the canonical cache repository as its source.
